# Independent Re-Verification — Vol2 Ch13–19 (post 2026-09-18 fix)

Date: 2026-09-18
Scope: `data/raw_pdf/CivilDAR_2019_Vol_2.pdf` pages (0-indexed) Ch13 (9–109), Ch14 (109–179), Ch15 (179–227), Ch16 (227–371), Ch17 (371–485), Ch18 (485–753), Ch19 (753–855), vs `data/reference_json/ch13_items.json` … `ch19_items.json` as they stand after the 2026-09-18 fix to `scripts/vol2_chapter_extractor.py`.

## Methodology

`scripts/vol2_chapter_extractor.py` was **not** read or trusted. A fresh script (pdfplumber, default settings) dumped raw per-page text for all seven chapters independently. Three separate independent-verification passes were then run against that raw text, each implemented from scratch with its own regexes:

1. **Item census** — built a header/leaf-item tree per chapter from raw text (any line starting `NN.d(.d)*`, excluding lines that are actually internal `"Rate as/same as per item No./item no."` cross-references), identified true leaf items (codes with no deeper child code), and for every leaf pulled the **last** `"Say <amount>"` line before the next header as the expected final unit rate. Compared this to `ch*.json` for presence and correctness.
2. **First-vs-last Say check** — for every leaf item, collected **every** `Say <amount>` occurrence in its block (including intermediate quantity-derivation annotations like `"= 0.43 cum Say 0.43 cum"`) and checked whether the JSON's `say` field matches the first or the last occurrence.
3. **Cross-reference resource-row and basis/unit checks** — regex-matched every `"<code> Rate as/same as per item No./no. ..."` resource line in the raw text per item and checked it is present in the item's JSON `resources`; separately matched `"Detail(s) of cost for <N|one|a> <unit>"` lines and checked JSON `basis`/`unit` against them.

All duplicate-code checks were exhaustive (every item in every chapter's JSON), not sampled.

## Missing items

Zero leaf items confirmed missing from the JSON across all 7 chapters in this independent re-verification (the full-census check in Methodology step 1 found 0 unmatched leaf-item codes in every chapter). This is a genuine improvement — the old audit's Ch16/Ch18 missing-item findings do not reproduce.

## What is confirmed solid

- The header-regex trap bug (cross-ref lines mistaken for item headers) that caused Ch18's ~20% corruption and Ch16's missing 16.10: **fixed**, verified exhaustively (duplicate-code census across all 556 Ch18 items and 143 Ch16 items) and specifically for every named item.
- Item presence/completeness at the item-code level: **fixed** — 0 missing leaf items found chapter-wide.
- Unit/basis metadata: **dramatically improved** (>97% correct in spot checks), though not 100%.
- 17.81's specific missing resource row (code 1966): **fixed**.

## What is not (fully) fixed

- A **new/previously-undetected** bug (Finding A) silently corrupts the `say` rate — the single most important field — for 8.6% of items chapter-wide, and **nearly half of Ch19**. This was not flagged in the 2026-09-17 audit (that audit's ~80-item sample apparently didn't hit this pattern) and is arguably more serious than the original header-trap bug because it produces no missing/duplicate signal — the item looks completely normal, just carries the wrong number.
- The multi-line-wrap / cross-ref-row-drop issue (old Finding #2/#3) is **not fully fixed** in Ch18, including in 4 of the 5 specifically re-checked "previously broken" items.

## Final verdict

**Does the 2026-09-18 fix hold up under independent re-verification for Vol2 Ch13–19? Partially.** The specific catastrophic bug it targeted — the header-regex trap that deleted and duplicate-corrupted items in Ch16/Ch18 — is convincingly fixed: exhaustive duplicate-code and missing-item checks came back clean across all 7 chapters, and all 5 specifically-flagged Ch18 items plus Ch16's 16.10 are now present and correctly coded. However, this re-verification surfaced a **new, severe, previously-undetected defect**: 120 items (8.6% chapter-wide, 47.6% of Ch19) carry a silently wrong `say` unit rate because the parser grabs an early intermediate quantity annotation instead of the item's true final rate. Ch18 also still drops ~55% of cross-reference resource rows and some multi-line-wrapped material rows when preceded by explanatory text, directly affecting 4 of the 5 previously-flagged items. **The raw PDF should continue to be retained**, and a further fix pass targeting the first-vs-last-Say selection logic (Finding A, highest priority — fixes the actual displayed rate) and the multi-line-preceded cross-reference row capture in Ch18 (Finding B) is recommended before Ch13–19 can be considered a reliable PDF replacement.
