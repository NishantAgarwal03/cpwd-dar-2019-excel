# Data notes

The workbooks were prepared from CPWD DAR 2019 PDF source material. Supplementary materials are available in the [Google Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1CNWrcDIm02sm1Ff3BocdSns7WgLXGj08?usp=sharing).

They are independent conversion artifacts, not official CPWD publications. They may contain transcription, interpretation, formula, or source-version differences. Users must independently verify items, units, rates, and calculations against the authoritative source.
